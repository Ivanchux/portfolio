Legal notice and privacy

The short version: this website uses no cookies, has no analytics and stores none of your data.

Who is behind it

This site is the personal portfolio of {{nombre_publico}}. It shows personal projects and has no commercial activity: it does not sell products or services.

Cookies and storage

There are no cookies and no visitor tracking tools. The website only remembers two of your preferences in your browser: light or dark mode and the colour of the monitor screen (green, amber or ice). That data never leaves your device and you can delete it by clearing the site data.

Fonts are hosted on the website itself: they are not loaded from third-party servers.

Contact

The contact form sends nothing by itself: it opens your email app with the message ready. Whatever you decide to send arrives by email and is only used to reply to you.

Live views and external links

Some projects can be viewed live inside the monitor. That view only loads if you press “View live”, and then your browser connects to the project's website (for example on GitHub Pages), which has its own terms. The same happens when you follow links to GitHub or other sites.
